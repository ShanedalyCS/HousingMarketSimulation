# Housing Market Simulation v1.0.0

Portfolio Release v1.0.0 presents the complete deterministic housing-market
simulation, analytical pipeline, and interactive dashboard as a publicly
deployable project.

## Highlights

- **Agent-based market engine:** heterogeneous buyer and property agents evolve
  through a reproducible monthly discrete-time lifecycle.
- **Affordability and preferences:** savings, salary, motivation, family status,
  and weighted property preferences shape each buyer's purchasing decisions.
- **Sealed-bid settlement:** affordability-capped bids settle through
  asking-price and second-price evidence with deterministic seeded tie-breaking.
- **Fundamental and comparable valuation:** cost-based property values blend
  with bounded, similarity-weighted completed-sale evidence.
- **Market feedback:** transactions influence later estimated values, seller
  targets, buyer perceptions, bids, and sale prices.
- **Monthly reporting:** operational market snapshots distinguish active stock,
  monthly activity, ending inventory, and price movement.
- **Reproducible scenarios:** balanced, excess-demand, and excess-supply markets
  run for 120 seeded months.
- **Constant-quality price index:** rolling median sale-price-to-`BaseValue`
  factors are independently rebased to 100 overall and by location.
- **Analytics exports:** stable invariant CSV and nullable JSON preserve missing
  evidence without fabricated zeroes or non-finite values.
- **Interactive dashboard:** a responsive, self-contained HTML/CSS/JavaScript
  dashboard compares price, supply, demand, activity, liquidity, affordability,
  and location results offline.
- **Automated verification:** 74 xUnit tests cover market rules, lifecycle
  timing, analytics methodology, deterministic replay, exports, and dashboard
  generation.
- **CI and deployment:** GitHub Actions performs Release builds and tests, then
  generates and deploys the static dashboard to GitHub Pages from `main`.

## Validated scenario results

| Scenario | Raw asking-price change | Quality-adjusted index | Sale-to-list ratio | Average time on market |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Balanced | -17.47% | 113.34 | 103.76% | 7.03 months |
| Excess demand | -25.14% | 115.80 | 107.79% | 2.07 months |
| Excess supply | -8.26% | 105.64 | 100.77% | 12.18 months |

Excess demand produces the strongest quality-adjusted pressure even though the
raw average of remaining listings falls. This divergence reflects changing
property composition rather than a claim that every listing depreciates.

## Known limitations

- Rules are explanatory and are not empirically calibrated forecasts.
- Financing repayments, interest rates, taxes, rentals, transaction costs, and
  construction pipelines are outside the model.
- Sellers use transparent persistent rules rather than strategic learning.
- The quality index is transaction-only and uses simulated `BaseValue`, not a
  real hedonic valuation.
- Sparse completed-sale samples can make location indices unavailable.

Quality-adjusted price index

Trailing 12-month median sale-price-to-BaseValue factor, independently rebased to 100 when enough evidence first exists.

Why raw averages can mislead

Raw average prices can change because the types of houses sold or remaining change. The constant-quality index controls for this by comparing each sale price with the house’s fundamental BaseValue. It is a simulation-specific benchmark, not an official hedonic index.

Raw prices versus quality-adjusted movement

Currency prices use the left axis; index points use the right axis. Gaps mean insufficient transactions, never a fabricated zero.

Supply and demand

Active buyers and listings are stocks; the ratio uses the right axis.

Market activity

Bids and completed transactions are monthly flows; clearance is transactions per active listing.

Liquidity

Months of supply is ending inventory divided by current-month transactions and is unavailable in zero-sale months.
